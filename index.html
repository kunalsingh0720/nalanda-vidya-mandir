About the school

A school named after a library

Nalanda, in the 5th century, drew scholars from Korea to Persia to a monastery in Bihar that housed one of the ancient world's largest libraries. We took the name deliberately: not as decoration, but as a standard to keep failing upward toward.

Our mission is plain — to raise learners who are confident without being certain, who can defend an idea and still change their mind, and who leave school as good at listening as they are at arguing.

"A school's real library is not its shelves but its questions. At Nalanda Vidya Mandir, we still ask them the old way — out loud, and to each other."

— Office of the Principal

Admissions

Five steps, start to enrolment

Admissions for 2027–28 are open. Lateral entry to Grades 6 and 9 is subject to seat availability.

1

Enquiry & campus visit

Reach out or walk in for a tour and a conversation with our admissions team.

2

Application

Submit the form with basic student and family details.

3

Interaction or assessment

An age-appropriate conversation for Nursery–Grade 2; a written assessment from Grade 3 onward.

4

Document verification

Birth certificate, previous school records where applicable, and address proof.

5

Confirmation

Offer letter, fee payment, and a welcome orientation before term begins.
